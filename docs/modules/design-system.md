# design system · ink on paper, on a soft canvas; hue = priority

- **Foundations (m78):** six text sizes as tokens (`text-display` 32 · `text-title` 22 · `text-heading` 17 · `text-body` 15 · `text-small` 13 · `text-micro` 11); one shape rule (`rounded-card` 16 px for a card on the canvas, 12 px for a well or row inside it, pills for buttons and chips); shadows tinted to the canvas and only on floating surfaces; an ink focus ring on every control; buttons press at 0.98; reduced motion honoured; no all-caps labels. Findings and phases: `docs/DESIGN_AUDIT.md`.

- **m76:** `/ui` is "Building blocks", described in plain words (how urgent, the numbers on the desk, one label per row, where each fact came from, the place circle, buttons, the list), and `make copy-check` covers it like every other screen.

- **What it is:** `apps/web/components/ui/` — the small set of pieces every screen is built from, shown together at **`/ui`** (local listener only). Tokens live in `app/globals.css` (`@theme`): ink, three warm greys, one hairline, a quiet wash, the canvas, paper, the four priority hues, amber, and two shadows.
- **The look (m60, D18):** white cards (20 px radius, a hairline, a whisper of shadow) on a warm light canvas; a sidebar (`components/shell/`: icons + labels, folding to an icon rail on the desk and to one row on a phone); round icon wells ([lucide](https://lucide.dev) icons, ISC licence, free); pill buttons; the greeting and today's date on Incidents, the date and time on the desk. Each priority also sits in a soft well of its own hue (`PriorityWell`), the only colour on screen. A broken promise is an ink ring and an ink icon, never a colour.
- **The rules it encodes (PRD §13.5):** **hue means priority and nothing else**, and every priority is also a plain word and a shape (`▲ Urgent · ◆ High · ● Medium · ○ Low`; the code P1…P4 only in the tooltip, m66); "needs you" is **ink inversion** (the attention chip); stale is **fade** (`.stale`); uncertainty is shown — `~6` estimates, `○` unchecked, **amber ○** only where the quote check failed (F-C13), dashed circle = Low.
- **Plain words (m66):** `lib/words.ts` is the glossary in code: the API keeps its short codes ("unchecked P1", "Assign → SDRF Boat 2 · 2.2 km · boat"), which tests and partners rely on, and screens say them in plain words ("not checked yet", "Send SDRF Boat 2 · 2.2 km away"). `make copy-check` refuses the banned jargon in shown text (docs/SIMPLIFY_PLAN.md).
- **Receipts:** `MethodChip` (AI · Rule · Person · Partner · Recording; AI in italics), `Check` (● / ○ / amber ○), `ChannelTag` (Voice · WhatsApp · Sheet · Typed at the desk · Partner system) and `Receipt`, the grey pipeline line under each report.
- **Because:** `Because` renders the reason line every decision carries ("because trapped + water entering").
- **Field buttons** (`size="field"`) are ≥ 64 px tall and full width, for the responder and reporter screens.
- **Caption:** the judge-legibility line (DEMO_MODE), one plain sentence from a template; it never speaks for the model.
- **Not used, on purpose:** charts, KPI tiles, trend arrows, coloured status dots, toasts and modals (PRD §13.5, the cut list). The promise bar is three cards of words and numbers that add up.
