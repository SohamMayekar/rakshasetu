# rescues · the team says what happened; the family is asked for nothing

- **The sheet** (`components/rescue/RescueSheet.tsx`, the same on the responder phone and on the desk): "How many people did you take?" (pre-filled with the reported headcount, − and +), then **one** of three big answers to "Is anyone still there?" — **No · Yes · Don't know**. Under "Couldn't do it?": **Nobody here**, **Couldn't reach** (water or current · road or bridge · night · other), **Other: tell officer**. Taps go through the phone's outbox (10 s Undo, then sent with an idempotency key); the team phone's own location at that moment is attached if the phone gives it within 3 s.
- **What each answer does** (`POST /v1/cases/{id}/rescues`): nobody left → **rescued**, and the **ticker closes it after the review window** (2 h; 20 s for live demo cases) with "Rescued 6 of 6 · Fire & Rescue Boat 5 · 14:32", unless something blocks it · someone still there (or "don't know" with fewer taken than reported) → **partial**: the case stays open for the rest, keeps its priority and goes back to the queue **with its original waiting time** — "2 of 6 still there", at the top of P1 (**T8**, tested) · nobody here → back as P1 ("search the circle") · couldn't reach → stays open, keeps its priority, and the next team sees why ("SDRF Boat 2 couldn't reach (road or bridge)") · other: tell officer → an officer task, and **every automated family message is suppressed**.
- **By radio:** an officer records the same sheet on the team's behalf ("Log radio report", with the channel): "Fire & Rescue Boat 5: 6 of 6 rescued, nobody left · via VHF by R. Das". The app's later sync of the same trip lands as **corroboration** (one rescue per assignment, enforced by the database).
- **Location check (P1 #3):** a record made more than the circle's radius + 1 km from the pin is flagged "recorded away from the reported location" and never auto-closes; an officer closes it with a reason. GPS can be spoofed: this raises the bar; it proves nothing.
- **Officer decisions:** Close · Cancel · Reopen, each with a reason (`case.decide`); a case a team still holds can't be closed.
