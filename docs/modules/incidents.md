# incidents and audit · the national layer, and the log anyone can verify

- **Incidents as data:** `POST /v1/incidents` (admin) creates an incident with its districts; reports name it by its code and **land with no code change** (tested: "AS" created by API, a report ingested, case **AS-1** made by the same pipeline, its own promise bar). `PATCH /v1/incidents/{id}` moves the lifecycle forward only (draft → active → standing down → closed; standing down can return to active).
- **The front door** (`/`, signed out, m64): two big choices only, **I need help** (→ `/r`) and **Control room dashboard** (→ `/login`), the team app as a small link underneath. Signed in, `/` sends a team to `/responder` and a desk user with one incident to that desk; only someone who sees several incidents (the Demo admin, a state viewer) stays on the Incidents screen.
- **The Incidents screen** (`/`): one card per incident the user may see, each with its own promise bar, and a map of India with the incidents (a filled dot when one of its promises is broken). One click opens that incident's desk. The SEOC viewer sees only its state's incidents, totals only. The overview map **doesn't draw international boundaries** (they come from OpenStreetMap, not the Survey of India).
- **The sidebar (m69):** at most three places: **Desk** (or **All incidents** for someone who sees several), **History** (this screen) and **Demo**; Surge and the partner log are two cards inside Demo ("Show it under load", "Show a partner system").
- **The Audit screen, now called History** (`/audit`, `audit.read`; `/audit?case=<id>` from the drawer): the hash-chained events, newest first (who, what, reason, hash), and **Check nothing was changed** (was Verify chain), which recomputes every hash; entry numbers and fingerprints are under Show details: "✓ 223 events verified", or the first broken link. **Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof:** the app's database role can't edit history; a superuser could re-hash everything, which is why `GET /v1/audit/head` is public for a partner to countersign.
